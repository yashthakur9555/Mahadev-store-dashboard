# Mahadev-store-dashboard

Objective of the Dashboard 

To monitor and analyze overall ecommerce performance by tracking sales, profit, quantity, customer behavior, and payment trends, enabling better decision-making across regions, categories, and time.

📊 Business Questions Solved by This Dashboard

💰 Sales & Profit Insights

What is the total revenue and profit generated?
Which months are most and least profitable?
Are there any loss-making periods (e.g., May, July, December)?

📍 Regional Performance

Which states generate the highest sales?
Where should the business focus expansion or marketing efforts?

👥 Customer Analysis

Who are the top customers contributing to revenue?
Is revenue concentrated among a few customers?

🛍️ Product & Category Insights

Which categories (Clothing, Electronics, Furniture) drive the most sales?
Which sub-categories generate the highest profit (e.g., Printers, Bookcases)?
Are there low-performing products that need attention?

💳 Payment Behavior

What are the most preferred payment methods (COD, UPI, Cards)?
How much revenue depends on Cash on Delivery (COD)?

📦 Order & Quantity Trends

How many total items are sold?
Which categories contribute most to order volume?
